# Bybit remaining market, earn and transfer coverage — 2026-09-21

Adds 350 asset translations, 59 network translations, 219 spot instruments and
171 linear perpetual instruments, backed by 324 new canonical assets and 57
blockchain records. Base: `2ad44d5`. Related: [Catalogue #130](https://github.com/tribulnation/catalogue/issues/130).

## Descriptions and official links

All 324 new assets and 57 new blockchain records include a
project-specific English description and at least one project, issuer,
documentation, source-code, community or verified token-contract link. Link labels identify their purpose.
Source review excludes parked domains and distinguishes similarly named projects;
shared records have identical metadata across all four venue PRs. This correction
changes no asset identity, translation or instrument fields.

## Identity evidence

1. Current [Bybit currency metadata](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/asset/coin-info)
   provides exact native currencies, chains and token contracts. Currency names
   usually repeat their tickers, so those names alone are not treated as identity
   evidence. Exact contract addresses on the same chain are joined to fully named
   projects in [KuCoin currency metadata](https://api.kucoin.com/api/v3/currencies),
   or to existing verified Catalogue on-chain translations. Ethereum addresses
   compare case-insensitively; non-EVM identifiers preserve case. This change adds
   exchange translations, not new on-chain contract translations.
2. Current [Bybit linear instrument metadata](https://api.bybit.com/v5/market/instruments-info?category=linear&limit=1000)
   also supplies `fullName`, `symbolType`, base, quote and settlement currencies.
   Fully named projects corroborate currency identities. Native-chain cases use
   the matching Bybit and KuCoin chain/currency definitions. Shared canonical
   records are reused across the venue PRs; variant native tickers remain local.
3. [GRAM replaces TON one-to-one](https://announcements.bybit.com/en/article/bybit-toncoin-ton-gram-gram--blt617f4bf48950ab77/),
   so it resolves to `toncoin`. [DATA replaces Story/IP one-to-one](https://announcements.bybit.com/en/article/-story-ip-data-network-data--blt2eb534d1e37d3aad/),
   so it resolves to `story`, not Streamr. Other collisions remain distinct:
   Bybit `MET` is Meteora; `VELO` is Velo; `U` is Union (KuCoin `UNION`, not
   KuCoin's United Stables); `EDGE` is edgeX, not Definitive.
4. Existing wrapped/stablecoin identities are reused only with exact contract
   evidence. xStocks remain tokenized claims separate from the underlying shares.
   New canonical records without verified external identifiers deliberately omit
   those optional identifiers; no price fallback or peg is inferred.

## Instruments and networks

Only currently trading spot and linear perpetual contracts exposed by the Bybit
SDK are added. Dated futures, inverse contracts and prelisting instruments are
excluded. Newly added crypto instruments retain exact venue market IDs and
`spot`/`perp` exchange IDs. This pass does not expand the stock, ETF, commodity or
forex perpetual universe.

Nine added basket-unit perpetuals carry explicit instrument multipliers (1,000,
10,000 or 1,000,000), with no scaled global currency aliases. Fresh Bybit spot tickers (eight markets) and KuCoin spot (TURBO), compared
with Bybit perpetual tickers, independently confirm the units: after applying the multiplier,
price ratios range from 0.9937 to 1.0045. Contract lot sizes are not used as price
multipliers. Instrument URLs are generated by the repository script.

Networks are identified separately from token assets. Polkadot Asset Hub and
Avalanche X-Chain reuse their distinct blockchain records. Both `ZETA` and
`ZETAEVM` identify the [same Cosmos-based EVM blockchain](https://www.zetachain.com/blog/introducing-zetachain).
New records without independently qualified namespace/chain IDs omit those
optional fields. These remaining native routes stay unresolved:

1. `ENJIN`: Bybit's label does not distinguish Matrixchain from Relaychain.
2. `DYMEVM`: the EVM route has not been qualified relative to the Dymension Hub.
3. `MATIC.E`: a bridged-USDC route requires asset identity handling; a network-only
   alias would leave the primary asset incorrectly normalized as native USDC.
4. `XION`: currency metadata mixes XION and Verona Chain; migration continuity
   needs further evidence.
